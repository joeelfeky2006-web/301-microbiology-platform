Brand idea — the Atlas Lens

  1. The lens. One ring reads as a microscope lens, a petri dish and a globe (the "atlas").
  2. The pulse M. A clinical trace that spells M, with two peaks that echo the Giza pyramids.
  3. The capstone. A teal node on the summit, the AI spark at the top of your studying.

Personality & voice

Clinical, but never cold.

Clinical-premium in accuracy and layout. Friendly in tone. Sharp-tech in how AI is shown. Logos stay English. Ad copy mixes Egyptian Arabic with the English medical terms students already use.

Logo set

Clear space is at least the capstone diameter on every side. Minimum size: horizontal logo 120 px wide, symbol 20 px (use the favicon cut below 32 px). Use Color on light backgrounds, Reversed on dark, and Mono on photos or single-colour print. The Micro 301 lockup is the beta product badge and never replaces the MedAtlas logo.
